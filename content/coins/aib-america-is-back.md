## Why the Vamp Won

**Platform narrative alignment:** Trump's own memecoin ($TRUMP) launched on Bonk's infrastructure. Degens knew this. When AIB appeared on pump.fun, a faction immediately argued the "correct" version should be on Bonk — the platform associated with Trump in crypto. This gave the Bonk AIB a legitimacy argument that resonated.

**Capital concentration:** Once the narrative split, capital had to choose. The Bonk AIB argument was stronger (platform alignment > first-mover), so the majority of new money flowed there.

**Source credibility match:** Both the pump.fun original and Bonk AIB were linked to @RT_com (3.51M) in Axiom's Twitter alerts — equal source credibility. The third "OG" coin was promoted by @ferozwala (13.1K) — much weaker, which is why it died fastest.

**The "OG" argument failed:** Claiming you're "the OG" without a real legitimacy backing is the weakest vamp position. No platform story, no credible promoter, and competing with two already-established coins for the same narrative.

## Lessons

1. **Political figures generate faster, more competitive launches than AI figures.** Trump's name = immediate multi-coin race. Sam Altman = orderly single-coin launch.

2. **Platform narrative alignment is the tie-breaker in vamping scenarios.** The coin whose platform matches the narrative context wins. Trump + Bonk = Bonk AIB wins. Had Trump's slogan been about AI, a pump.fun version might have dominated.

3. **First mover ≠ winner in vamping.** pump.fun AIB migrated first but still lost. Speed of migration only matters when there's no competing legitimacy claim.

4. **"OG" is the weakest vamp argument.** It's entirely circular ("we're the real one because we said so"). Always loses to a specific, externally grounded claim (platform alignment, official endorsement).

5. **When vamping is detected, exit the wrong coin immediately.** Holding the losing vamp while the winning vamp runs is how you turn a potential win into a loss.

6. **Source follower count matters for OG vs vamp.** Both main coins were linked to @RT_com (3.51M). The OG Bonk was linked to @ferozwala (13.1K) — that's why it died. A vamp needs at least equal source credibility to survive.
