## New Trigger Type: Video-Embedded Name

**What it is:** The token name comes from spoken word or visual text inside a video attached to a tweet — not from the tweet text itself.

**Why it's different:**
- Requires someone to watch/listen to the video, not just read the tweet
- Bots cannot auto-parse video audio — no automated token launch in < 13 seconds
- Propagation is slower: word spreads on CT reply chains as people who watched it point it out
- The slower spread = **wider entry window than text-based triggers**

**Detection:**
```
1. See a tweet from a high-credibility account with video attached
2. Tweet text doesn't contain an obvious memeable word
3. Watch the video for any named entity (person, pet, object, place)
4. Check pump.fun for that name immediately if found
```

**Edge:** Most participants spot this via CT replies ("she named the pig Chloe lmaooo") rather than watching the video themselves. By the time that reply spreads, the BC is partway filled — but the window is longer than text triggers.

## Lessons

1. **Video-embedded names create wider entry windows.** If the trigger is spoken in a video rather than written in tweet text, propagation is slower. You have more time to enter than in a text-based trigger.

2. **Solana advisor status is a credibility multiplier.** Nikita Bier's @solana advisory role made this feel "native" to the ecosystem. Degens assigned higher legitimacy than they would to a generic exec.

3. **No vamping = concentrated capital = higher ATH.** Chloe's $5.56M into one coin produced a $1.68M ATH. AIB's $5.09M split three ways produced a $658K ATH on the winner. The capital concentration effect is real and measurable.

4. **Bundler % is a risk flag, not a kill switch.** 15.5% bundler allocation is elevated, but the narrative overwhelmed it. Track this signal as a risk modifier — size down, don't skip.

5. **clukzsol's ladder-exit model.** The top trader made 64 sells on the way up, not one exit. This approach captures more of the move than a single target exit and avoids getting caught at the top.

6. **The 1M follower milestone is its own hook.** Nikita's tweet was credible partly because it was a documented milestone moment. Milestone tweets attract more engagement and more eyes than random posts — increasing spread speed.
